LeaseIt is a two-sided marketplace for University of Washington students to sublease housing and transfer furniture or household items, with @uw.edu verification and peer-to-peer matching.<br>
<br>
Built on the MERN stack with Stripe escrow, DocuSign contracts, Socket.IO chat, Cloudinary media, and GPT-4/Groq helpers for listing copy, pricing tips, and moderation.
