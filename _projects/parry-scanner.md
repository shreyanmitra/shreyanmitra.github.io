Parry Scanner is the Valid8 enterprise SAST codebase: multi-language static analysis with tree-sitter/AST tooling, confidence controls, and ML-assisted false-positive reduction aimed at high-precision scans at scale.<br>
<br>
The Python project ships a FastAPI service, Docker/Kubernetes deploy paths, CI integrations, and observability hooks (Prometheus/OpenTelemetry), spanning 15+ languages and library-aware checks.
