Centuries of Conflict visualizes historical battle data from the CDB90 dataset and Wikidata (via SPARQL), exploring where conflicts occurred, how costly they were, and which battles were recorded.<br>
<br>
Built with D3 for CSE 442 at the University of Washington, the project uses maps, stream graphs, and linked charts so viewers can compare duration, front width, and casualty patterns across centuries. Live demo: https://shreyanmitra.github.io/centuries-of-conflict/
