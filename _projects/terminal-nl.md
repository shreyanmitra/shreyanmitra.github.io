Terminal-NL turns constrained English (“Go to Downloads”, “Delete folder temp”) into typed intents, validates them, renders PowerShell or POSIX previews, and executes with a safety layer—separating language from shell syntax.<br>
<br>
The Python package includes lexer/parser/intent/os-adapter/executor modules, a small proprietary scripting language (variables, conditionals, loops, filesystem ops), and tests aimed at teaching classic compiler-style pipelines.
