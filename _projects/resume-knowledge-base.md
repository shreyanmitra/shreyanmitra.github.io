Resume Knowledge Base lets students store projects, experiences, and stories once, then generate grounded resume drafts, interview answers, recruiter emails, and portfolio copy ranked against a target job description.<br>
<br>
The app is dependency-light, runs in the browser with local storage (plus JSON import/export), and uses a deterministic generation engine designed so an LLM can be swapped in later without changing the product flow.
