Crunch helps students place tasks into real free slots using deadlines, priority, and estimated duration, with drag-and-drop reorganization and a time-efficiency score.<br>
<br>
The stack is React/TypeScript on the frontend and Node/Express on the backend, with Google Calendar and Microsoft Graph sync plus an LLM agent that can create, move, and plan calendar events from natural language.
