Co-op Critter Care helps groups share responsibility for a pet with concurrent to-do lists, reminders, and a care history that updates across users. Live site: https://www.coopcrittercare.com/<br>
<br>
Built as a TypeScript/Next.js app with Tailwind and Vitest, it supports shared pet workspaces and task check-offs for feeding, medication, and other recurring care.
